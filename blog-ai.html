AI and Websites: What AI Can Do, and What It Cannot Replace

Shakil Khan • October 2026 • AI

AI has changed how websites are built. It can write code, draft text, generate images and suggest layouts in seconds. Used well, it makes a good developer faster. Used blindly, it makes a bad website faster too.

What AI is genuinely good at

  • First drafts: copy, code structure and design ideas to start from, not to publish as-is.
  • Repetitive work: resizing images, cleaning code, writing routine sections.
  • Ideas and options: quickly showing different directions before you commit to one.

What AI cannot replace

  • Understanding your business: AI does not know your customers, your prices, or what makes a visitor trust you.
  • Finding the real cause: when a site is slow or broken, AI can guess — a developer investigates, tests and proves the cause.
  • Judgement: what to keep simple, what to remove, and what will actually bring customers, not just look modern.

The right way to use AI on a website

Use AI as an assistant, not as the builder. Let it speed up the routine work, then let a human check every word, every image and every button against one question: does this help a real visitor take the next step? A website built entirely by AI often looks fine and converts poorly, because nobody asked that question.

The bottom line: AI is a powerful tool in the workshop. The craft — understanding the business, finding the cause, and building trust — still belongs to people.
