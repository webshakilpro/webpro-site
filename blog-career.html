How to Start a Career in Website Development (Without Wasting Years)

Shakil Khan • October 2026 • Career

You do not need a computer science degree to build websites for a living. You need the right skills, in the right order, and real work to show. Here is the path I would recommend to anyone starting today.

Step 1: Learn the basics properly

Start with HTML and CSS — how a page is structured and styled. Then learn WordPress properly: how themes, plugins, pages and posts actually work. This foundation matters more than any single tool, because tools change and basics do not.

Step 2: Learn one builder deeply

Pick Elementor and learn it well, along with WooCommerce for shops and a tool like Crocoblock or ACF for custom content. One deep skill that solves real business problems is worth more than ten shallow ones.

Step 3: Build 3–4 demo projects

Build a business website, a shop, a booking site and a landing page — as demo samples, clearly labelled. Clients hire from what they can see, not from certificates. Your portfolio is your proof.

Step 4: Learn to fix, not just build

Bug fixing and speed optimisation are where many beginners become professionals. Learn to find the cause first: check plugins, images, hosting and caching before changing anything. Clients remember the person who fixed their site properly.

Step 5: Get your first clients honestly

Start with small, local or freelance jobs, deliver on time, and communicate clearly. Do not promise what you cannot do, and do not copy other people's work. Reputation grows slowly — and it is the only marketing that lasts.

The bottom line: learn basics, go deep on one stack, build visible demos, learn to fix, and be honest with clients. That is the whole career path, one project at a time.
